# Solution Lab 07 — Agentic AI Solution Architecture

**Created by School of AI**

## Customer
Apex Global is a 25,000-user multinational enterprise operating in the US and EU with CRM, service desk, document, data warehouse and enterprise identity systems.

## Outcome
**Design a bounded agentic solution and explain where deterministic workflow remains preferable.**

## Step-by-step
1. Extract solution drivers.
2. Define functional requirements.
3. Define at least eight NFRs.
4. Identify data and integration dependencies.
5. Choose the solution pattern.
6. Draw the logical architecture.
7. Mark trust boundaries.
8. Design failure behavior.
9. Define evaluation and operational metrics.
10. Estimate cost drivers.
11. Compare at least two alternatives.
12. Map to cloud services when applicable.
13. Create an ADR.
14. Produce an implementation handoff checklist.
15. Defend the design in five minutes.
